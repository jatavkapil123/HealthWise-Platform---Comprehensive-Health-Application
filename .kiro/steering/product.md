# HealthWise Product Overview

HealthWise is a comprehensive digital health platform that combines AI-powered diagnostics, telemedicine, and wellness solutions to address real-world healthcare challenges.

## Core Features

- **User Authentication**: JWT-based login/register system with password reset functionality
- **Lab Test Booking**: Book pathology and radiology tests with home collection options
- **Doctor Consultations**: Connect with certified doctors through secure video/audio consultations
- **Medicine Ordering**: Order prescription and OTC medicines with delivery tracking
- **Health Reports**: Digital health records and medical history management
- **Health Alerts**: Proactive health monitoring with vital signs tracking, medication reminders, and emergency alerts
- **Health Library**: Curated health content, web stories, and educational resources
- **Emergency Care**: 24/7 emergency support with one-click access

## User Roles

- **Patient**: Regular users who book services and manage their health
- **Doctor**: Healthcare providers who conduct consultations
- **Admin**: Platform administrators with full access

- Improve healthcare accessibility in remote/rural areas
- Provide AI-powered health insights and recommendations
- Enable proactive health monitoring and early disease detection
- Reduce medication errors through smart reminders
- Build supportive health communities
